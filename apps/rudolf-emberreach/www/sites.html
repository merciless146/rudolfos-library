Sites on the ground: draft layouts and models

Each kind of site from sites.js (L4.1), laid out from a seed: the plan from above (plazas with their approach cones, the vault, the structures, guardians, loose ground in red), and the model from an oblique camera, from the meshes the game will draw. Ruin fields in each of the five styles a people may build in; an outlying waystation; a Precursor site; an old hulk and a fresh wreck.
